## What the deck covers

1. The problem, in four cited numbers
2. What the app does
3. How one check runs
4. Why these models, with the trade-off of each
5. No single point of failure
6. Privacy, including what does leave the device
7. What we measured
8. Where it goes next

## How the video was made

The interface in the video is the real one. Each block of the running app was captured as its own layer, so pieces move on their own and nothing is a redrawn mock. The phone shots at the start and end were rendered in Blender; everything in between was built in After Effects.

## Keeping it honest

- "Two AI agents" means two models with separate roles. Nothing acts on its own.
- Falcon-H1 is built in Abu Dhabi and multilingual. We say "built in Abu Dhabi", not "an Arabic model".
- 91.5% is the address-check layer on a blind set of 141 addresses. The full pipeline has not been measured yet, and both the deck and the video say so.
- The TAMM integration is a concept and is labelled as one.
- The SMS that opens the video is our own illustration of a scam message.
